# Troubleshooting log

Record observed failures and verified mitigations here. Keep raw logs private until they have been checked for serial numbers, hardware UUIDs, MAC addresses, BSSIDs, network names, usernames, tokens, and other identifying or sensitive data.

Use one entry per reproducible issue. Separate observations from hypotheses and confirmed root causes.
